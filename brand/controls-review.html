Contact details · illustrative

Inline links keep the body type and a visible underline. Plain booking references keep their body styling. Phone and email links open a dialer or compose window after a tap.

One neutral interaction language

Rest

Text colour, transparent fill.

Pressed

10% Text wash, square bounds.

Keyboard focus

2 px Text outline, 3 px offset.

Hit areas. Standalone links and controls have at least a 44 × 44 px target. Inline prose links keep their line flow; separate them into rows when space allows.
Colour. Reserve Signal for its five existing roles. Disclosure, links and ordinary navigation use Text. Disabled controls use Dim and have no press effect.
Feedback. Use a 6% Text wash on hover and a 10% wash on press. Corners stay square. The link underline remains visible in each state.
Opening. Keep the encoded Google Maps search URL. HTTPS opens in a browser; phone and email links open external apps. Opening requires a tap.
Failure. Keep the address visible and show “Could not open this link.” Keep this feedback in the current view.
Accessibility. Announce expanded/collapsed state and the external destination. Wrap long content at 320 px and enlarged text. Reduce decorative motion when requested.

Approval scope: review the disclosure, address block and link styles before app changes. The address stays inside the expanded card, aligned with its other fields. The link opens Google Maps after a tap.
