Minder

Purpose

Minder is a personal assistant for tasks, lists, reminders and schedules. The interface is black and red. Each element shows information or gives an action.

A

Information first

Each element shows information or gives an action. The edge column shows a count or a time. The brackets show the next step.

B

One accent

Signal red shows state and action. All other parts are black, white or grey.

C

Wide and angular

The design uses corner brackets, a vertical edge column, wide letters and one cut corner on the main button.

08/09Voice

Voice

Write short, direct sentences in English.

FieldRuleExample
TitleUse one or two words. State the situation.Today
LabelUse uppercase. Name the kind of information.NEXT STEP · 2 MIN
BodyWrite one sentence in sentence case. Give the next step and the time it takes.Open the tax portal and log in.
ButtonsUse one word.Done

Writing style

Write at about 80% of ASD-STE100. This applies to all text the user reads: notifications, Telegram messages, digests, error messages and the interface. Use sentences of 20 words or fewer. Use the active voice and simple tenses. Use one word for one meaning. Give instructions as commands. The exact rules: to check against the official ASD-STE100 specification.
